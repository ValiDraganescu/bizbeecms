# CMS v2.8.0

_2026-08-18 · changes since r-2.7.0_

## Features
- **Custom domains: certificates are now fully automatic.** A customer only adds the routing DNS record (CNAME to `cf.bizbeecms.com`, or the apex A records). As soon as it resolves to us, Cloudflare validates the domain over HTTP, issues the HTTPS certificate and renews it forever — there are no more `_acme-challenge` TXT or DCV-delegation records to add, and no "validate your domain to renew" emails.
- The Site's **Custom domain** panel now shows the certificate state (active / pending) with a **Check status** button instead of validation records.

## Other
- Domains attached before this release are upgraded to the automatic flow on their next redeploy or when re-checked from the panel.
- No CMS runtime changes in this release; the CMS bundle is identical to 2.7.0.
